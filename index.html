How Montessori learning works

Maria Montessori built her method on careful observation of children. The ideas below shape everything we do with them.

Child-led learning

Children choose activities that interest them and work at their own pace, which builds focus and a real wish to learn.

Hands-on materials

Counting rods, sandpaper letters and shape puzzles let a child touch an idea before they meet it on paper.

A prepared space

Low shelves, simple trays and a tidy layout let children reach what they need and put it back themselves.

Growing independence

Pouring, dressing, tidying and caring for the room give small children quiet confidence in what they can do.
